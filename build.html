2 Artworks

Drop everything the students handed in here, all at once.
Hand-in files from the student page, loose photos and videos, whole folders, or the zip Schoology gives you. The builder sorts it out.
What goes online: the title, artist label and statement shown here, a 1200 px copy of each image, and the video. File names, photo location data and camera details are erased first. Galleries are public web pages, so use a first name and last initial, and check media consent.
