Living Art demo

  1. Keep this page open on a laptop or projector, with one artwork showing on the right.
  2. Scan this code with a phone or iPad and tap Start the camera.
  3. Point it at the artwork on the right, from about an arm's length, so the whole piece fits in view.
  4. Press Next here to try the other two pieces.

Turn the screen brightness up and avoid reflections. No app to install, and nothing is recorded: the camera image never leaves the phone.
