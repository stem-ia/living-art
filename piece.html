How to make itSee the demo

Get your piece ready here. Nothing is uploaded: your photo and video stay on this device until you hand in the file at the end.
Welcome back. Your work from last time is still here.

1 Photo of your artwork do this before you animate

Take a photo of your finished artwork, or choose one.
Straight on, even light, no glare, the whole piece in the shot.

    Drag the four gold dots onto the four corners of your artwork. The page crops to them and straightens the picture.

      Animate on top of this exact image. Save it, then bring it into your animation app as the first frame or the bottom layer. Your video then starts on your finished artwork and has the right shape.

      2 Your animation

      Add the video of your artwork coming alive.
      5 to 15 seconds. It starts on your finished artwork, then things move.

        3 Try it

        See what visitors will see. Point this device's camera at your real artwork, a print of it, or the photo on another screen.

        If the animation looks stretched or jumps when it starts, fix the video in step 2 and try again.

        4 Hand it in

          Only your title, your first name and last initial, your statement, the artwork image and the video go into the file. Phone location data is erased.
